How it works
  1. Every Digimon starts as a Baby. Click a card to see what it can digivolve into.
  2. Click the circle at the bottom right of a card to mark it as obtained. Your progress is saved in this browser.
  3. Under the name, the list shows the conditions your partner needs to digivolve into that Digimon. Stars (★) are its key points.
  4. Looking for a specific Digimon? Use the search to see its full evolution line.
